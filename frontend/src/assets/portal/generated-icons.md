# Ícones ilustrados dos portais

Criados em 27/11/2026 com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` (modo built-in), com transparência. Os WebP de produção preservam o canal alfa e foram otimizados para 512 × 512 px.

Arquivos finais: `network-v1.webp`, `reservations-v1.webp`, `sharing-v1.webp`, nesta pasta. Uso decorativo em `PortalArtwork.tsx`; os ícones pequenos de navegação continuam vetoriais para manter a leitura.

## Prompt comum

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one premium miniature 3D UI illustration for the Vagafogo nature tourism partner portal. Square composition, a single centered compact symbolic object, isolated on a genuinely transparent background, generous empty margins. Matte ceramic and softly brushed metal, sculptural minimal design, rich deep forest green #173D30 with warm champagne accents #D8C398 and ivory, gentle studio lighting from upper left, subtle ambient grounding shadow only close to object. Modern sophisticated hospitality dashboard, legible silhouette at 72px. No typography, no letters, no logos, no border, no tile or background panel, no decorative confetti, no photo.

## Complementos por arquivo

- `network-v1.webp`: Subject: a refined small architectural agency storefront shape with three rounded people figures connected below by a simple curved branch, expressing agency and seller network. Keep only 3 clear rounded figurines and one abstract arched doorway above.
- `reservations-v1.webp`: Subject: one elegant ivory admission ticket with a softly rounded forest green calendar card and one small champagne checkmark in front. The ticket has a subtle edge perforation. Simple compact reservation confirmation metaphor, no numbers or text.
- `sharing-v1.webp`: Subject: two sculptural interlocking forest green chain links with a small champagne leaf growing from one link, representing a referral link and a nature partnership. Two clear rounded links only, minimal elegant composition.
